# Working together, with respect.

These starting points are intended to help models, photographers and other creatives have clearer conversations. They are draft guidance for discussion, not an established PurplePort policy.

## Before you agree

Talk through the concept, location, timings, participants, compensation and any expenses. Discuss the intended level of work and any boundaries, and make sure everyone understands what they are agreeing to.

Agree how images may be selected, edited, credited and used, including any limits or permissions, before the shoot. Put important details in writing so everyone can refer back to them.

## During the collaboration

Treat everyone professionally and with respect. Ask before touching someone, changing the agreed approach or sharing behind-the-scenes material. A person can withdraw consent or ask to stop; take that seriously and discuss what happens next.

## After the shoot

Follow through on agreed deliverables and timelines. If something changes, communicate promptly. Respect agreed limits on editing, publication and sharing.

## If something feels wrong

You do not have to continue a situation that feels unsafe. Leave if you need to, seek support from someone you trust, and use the relevant platform’s reporting process where appropriate. In an emergency, contact the emergency services.

This is a starting draft. Details, terminology and any formal policies should be reviewed with the community before publication.
